\section{Risk Classifier: Regression Corpus and Stated Blind Spots}
\label{sec:app:classifier}

The risk tiers of Section~\ref{sec:method:immersive} rest on a lexical
classifier, not on a model. To keep that classifier from drifting silently we
wrote a pre-registered regression corpus and a two-implementation parity check;
the status below is the measurement instrument's, not a quality claim.

\paragraph{Corpus and gates.} \texttt{eval-set/\allowbreak{}risk-\allowbreak{}classifier-\allowbreak{}v1.\allowbreak{}jsonl} has
138 synthetic rows covering six intended categories plus a 12-row borderline
set (benign normal 22, benign role-play 21, benign joke 12, mild role-play 33,
severe override 20, severe red-line 18, borderline 12). Four gates must pass:
schema validity, severe recall $=1.0$, benign false-positive rate $=0.0$ on the
corpus, and zero Python--JavaScript mismatches in the contract fields
(\texttt{tier}, \texttt{trust\_exempt}). All four pass: confusion matrix
\texttt{none}\,62 / \texttt{mild}\,36 / \texttt{severe}\,40, per-tier precision
and recall 1.00, and parity $138/138$ (the non-contract field \texttt{emotion}
differs on 4 mild rows, where the two implementations use different confusion
cues; this is listed, not gated).

\paragraph{What the corpus does not cover.} The 138 rows are curated, so the
1.00 figures are a regression lock, not an estimate on real traffic. A separate
eleven-probe blind-spot set is therefore kept outside the corpus: after the
authorised vocabulary amendment, three probes are resolved and \textbf{eight
remain open}. They include benign false positives from word-level matching
(a literary discussion of ``murder methods'' is tiered severe; a historical use
of ``jail system'' can trip a jailbreak cue), a JavaScript-only false positive
on the phrase ``you are now'' (the Python side has no such pattern), and severe
misses where a paraphrase is absent from both vocabularies (``no restrictions
whatsoever''). We keep the corpus and the blind-spot set separate on purpose:
folding known failures into the regression lock would make the gate pass by
construction.

\paragraph{Honest status.} The classifier is a stub and the thresholds
(\texttt{escalateAfterSoftHits}, \texttt{softBufferMaxIntensity}) are design
defaults with provenance \texttt{guess}. The corpus establishes that the two
implementations agree on the contract fields and that the gate does not regress;
it does not establish a false-positive or false-negative rate on user traffic.
Those eight open probes are the concrete to-do list for calibration, and the
paper's safety claims do not depend on them: the cases in
Section~\ref{sec:exp:immersive} exercise the tiers, not the classifier's
accuracy.
